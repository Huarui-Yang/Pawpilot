Behind the build 🐾

Product and niche

PawPilot is a proposed €49/month management tool for small independent pet daycare and boarding businesses. The working alpha currently demonstrates dog daycare operations. Cat-specific features are planned, not built.

Alpha test

The first alpha opened but the buttons did not work. I rebuilt it with functioning navigation and quicker yes/no choices for nap and meal updates. I checked the interactive features on desktop.

Assumptions and honesty

€49/month is a proposed price from Task 1, not an active subscription. The waiting list is a front-end demonstration only: it does not collect or transmit emails. No customer ratings, testimonials or adoption claims are used.

Care and operational discipline

The product concept supports consistent check-in routines, visible vaccination reminders, daily pet activity records and capacity awareness. It does not replace staff training or welfare procedures.

Design and imagery

Buddy and the kitten imagery were AI-generated for this student project. All business claims are limited to the current dog-daycare alpha or clearly marked as planned.
