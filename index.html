Every pet has its own routine. Your staff should have the time and space to notice the little things: a quiet rest, a missed meal, or a nervous first day.

Care with structure: consistent check-in routines, visible safety reminders, clear activity logs and organised records help teams work responsibly.

PawPilot supports staff workflows; it does not replace professional judgement, supervision or a business's own welfare policies.
